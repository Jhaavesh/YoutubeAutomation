## 🎯 Rule 1: Strict Zero Repetition & Source Deduplication (Har Video 100% Unique)
- **Source Video ID Tracking:** Script har downloaded video ki YouTube ID (`used_source_ids`) ko database me track karegi. Agar koi ID pehle kabhi bhi use hui hai, script use **turant skip** karke agla fresh result uthayegi.
- **No Same Background Footage:** Kabhi bhi ek hi video clip par do alag text overlay nahi daale jayenge. Har Short ke liye alag footage aur alag scene hoga.
- Har video ke liye rotating unique categories:
  1. **GTA 6 Vice City Supercar Pursuits & Drifts**
  2. **GTA 6 vs GTA 5 Graphics & Physics Evolution**

## 🎵 Rule 2: Dynamic & Natural Sound Matching

## 👁️ Rule 3: Visual Hook & Modern Floating Typography (Zero Black Boxes)
- **Modern Clean Floating Text:** Black rectangular solid boxes (`drawbox`) ko permanently band kar diya gaya hai.

- **High Retention Duration:** 26 seconds per Short for maximum watch time and Shorts algorithm boost.
- Har video ke pehle 2 seconds mein screen par bold, engaging Hook text aayega taaki viewer turant ruk jaye.
